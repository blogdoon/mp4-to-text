MP4 → Text

Convert audio & video to text — 100% in your browser, your files never leave your machine.

Usage instructions:
  • Select an audio or video file to transcribe (sample: jfk.wav) — the model loads automatically on your first Transcribe click (57 MB, cached after that). Documents like .doc/.pdf don't work — audio/video only.
  • Click "Transcribe". The transcript appears below — use Copy or Download .txt to save it. Progress is autosaved every minute: after a crash, press Transcribe again to resume.
Requirements & other models
  • your browser must support WASM SIMD instructions for this to work
  • Firefox cannot load files larger than 256 MB - use Chrome instead
